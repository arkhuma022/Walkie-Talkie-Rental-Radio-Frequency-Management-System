# Walkie Rental System

A Django-based walkie-talkie rental and radio communication management system.

The system helps manage radio inventory, customer rentals, repeater infrastructure, frequency programming, payments, and emergency alerts.

---

# Features

## Customer Management
- Store customer details
- Company and individual profiles
- Rental history tracking
- Contact management

## Radio Inventory
- Walkie-talkie tracking
- Analogue radio support
- DMR digital radio support
- PoC SIM-card radio support
- Serial number tracking
- Maintenance tracking

## Rental Management
- Create rentals
- Assign radios
- Return tracking
- Overdue rentals
- Deposit tracking

## Payments
- Cash payments
- EFT payments
- Card payments
- Deposit management
- Rental fee tracking

## Repeater Management
- Repeater site records
- Tower tracking
- Antenna information
- Maintenance logs
- Online/offline monitoring

## Frequency Management
- RX/TX frequencies
- Color codes
- Talk groups
- Time slots
- Channel programming

## Alerts
- SOS alerts
- Offline radio alerts
- Maintenance alerts
- Emergency tracking

## Reports Dashboard
- Total customers
- Total radios
- Active rentals
- Available radios
- Overdue rentals
- Payment summaries
